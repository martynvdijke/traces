Account · Data deletion

Delete your TRACES account

You can delete your account and the memories stored with it at any time. This page explains how to do it, what is removed, and what may remain.

Last updated: November 2026

Deletion is permanent. Once your account is deleted, your memories and uploaded media cannot be recovered from the live service.

There is no form on this page. Use one of the three routes below.

01 How to delete your account

Choose the route that works for you. The Android app and the website use the same account, so deleting in either place deletes the same data.

Android app

In the TRACES app

  1. Open TRACES and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Account.
  3. Choose Delete account.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm.

If you cannot find Settings, update the app to the latest version from Google Play.

Web browser

On this website

  1. Sign in to your account.
  2. Open Settings → Account.
  3. Choose Delete account.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm.

You must be signed in to delete from the web. If you cannot sign in, use email instead.

Email fallback

If you cannot sign in

Email the operator with the subject “Account deletion request”. Write from the email address linked to your account if you have one, and include your username.

The operator may ask for enough information to confirm that the account is yours before deleting it.

02 What is deleted

When an account is deleted, these are removed from the live service:

Profile

Your username, display name, email address, and password hash or OIDC sign-in link.

Memories

Titles, descriptions, dates, places, GPS coordinates, tags, and the people links attached to your events.

Uploaded media

Photos, video, and audio files you uploaded for your memories.

Sessions

All active sessions end, so anyone signed in as you loses access to the account.

03 What may remain

Deletion is not always the same as instant erasure everywhere. These limited copies may remain:

Shared people records

A person you added may also be referenced by another account. The basic record, such as a name, can stay so other accounts' memories remain intact. Your own memories and media about that person are deleted.

Rotated backups

Backup copies may contain your data until they expire. They are used only to restore the service after a failure, not to recreate deleted accounts.

Technical logs

Logs may still contain request metadata such as an IP address and timestamps. They rotate on a schedule and are not used to rebuild your account.

Aggregate analytics

If Umami analytics is enabled, aggregate page-view counts remain. They do not identify you.

For retention periods and your other rights, see the Privacy Policy.
