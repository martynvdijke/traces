02 What this server stores

TRACES only needs data that helps you record and revisit memories. The categories are:

Account data

Your username, display name, optional email address, and either a bcrypt password hash or an OIDC subject identifier when sign-in is handled by an identity provider.

Memories

Titles, descriptions, dates, places, GPS coordinates, photos, video, audio, tags, and the people you record.

Preferences

Display and privacy settings, theme, default view, and notification choices.

Sign-in session

A record of your signed-in session so you do not have to log in on every page.

Technical logs

IP address, user agent, timestamps, and request paths, kept to secure the service and diagnose faults.

Uploaded media is stored on storage attached to the server. It is served only to accounts that are allowed to see it.

03 Why it is stored, and on what legal basis

Under the GDPR, processing needs a legal basis. This instance relies on the following:

  • Performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b)) — to create your account, store your memories, and keep you signed in. Without this data the service cannot work.
  • Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)) — to secure the server, prevent abuse and rate-limit requests, debug faults, and keep audit logs. These interests are balanced against your rights; you can object (see section 08).
  • Consent (Article 6(1)(a)) — for optional analytics, push notifications, and third-party integrations. You can withdraw consent at any time; that does not affect processing that already happened.

No advertising. This server does not run advertising, build advertising profiles, or sell your data. Your memories are not shared with advertisers or data brokers.

04 Cookies and local storage

TRACES uses the smallest set of cookies it can:

  • session — essential. It is HttpOnly, so page scripts cannot read it. It keeps you signed in and is removed when it expires or you sign out.
  • CSRF and OIDC state cookies — short-lived security cookies that protect forms against cross-site request forgery and complete sign-in through an identity provider. They expire quickly.

There are no advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

If the operator has enabled Umami analytics, it is cookieless and records aggregate page views only. You can turn it off in the app under Settings → Privacy.

Your browser's local storage is used to remember your theme choice and, where analytics is enabled, your analytics preference. It is not shared with third parties.

05 Who else processes data

Depending on how this instance is configured, the operator may use the services below. Optional services are only contacted when they are enabled and you use the related feature.

  • Hosting provider — runs the server and stores the database and media.
  • Self-hosted Umami — aggregate, cookieless usage statistics, if enabled.
  • OpenTelemetry collector — technical traces and metrics for monitoring, if enabled.
  • SMTP mail provider — sends password-reset and notification email, if enabled.
  • OIDC identity provider (for example Authelia) — handles sign-in, if enabled.
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim — turns GPS coordinates into place names when you add or edit a memory with a location.
  • Gotify — delivers push notifications, if enabled.
  • Immich — imports photos from an Immich library, if enabled.
  • BoardGameGeek — looks up board game details, if enabled.

Each service receives only what it needs for that task. Data is not sold, rented, or shared for advertising.

07 How long data is kept

  • Account and memories — kept while your account exists.
  • Sessions — expire automatically after a period set by the operator, or when you sign out.
  • Deleting your account — removes your profile, memories, and uploaded media. See Delete your account.
  • Backups — rotated backups may keep copies for a limited period until they expire. They are used only to restore the service after a failure.
  • Logs — rotate on a schedule and are not kept indefinitely.

The operator sets the exact session, backup, and log periods. You can ask for the current values using the contact address above.

08 Your rights

If you are in the EEA or the UK, you have the right to:

  • access a copy of your data;
  • correct inaccurate data;
  • erase your data;
  • restrict or object to processing;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent.

You can exercise most of these directly:

  • Download or delete — in the app, open Settings → Data & privacy and choose download or delete.
  • Delete without signing in — use the public account deletion page for instructions, including an email fallback.
  • Anything else — email {{LEGAL_CONTROLLER_EMAIL}}. The operator may ask you to confirm that you control the account before acting.

Requests are handled within the time limits set by the GDPR. If you believe your data has been handled unlawfully, you can complain to the data protection authority in your country. In the EU, you can find yours through the European Data Protection Board.

09 Security

  • In transit — when this instance is served over HTTPS, traffic between your browser and the server is encrypted with TLS.
  • Passwords — stored as bcrypt hashes, never as plain text.
  • Sessions — carried in an HttpOnly cookie that page scripts cannot read.
  • Access control — every request checks that the signed-in account owns the memory or media it asks for.
  • Operator access — limited to the operator and anyone they authorise to administer the server. They can read the database, so use an account you are comfortable with on this instance.

No system is perfectly secure. If you find a vulnerability, report it to {{LEGAL_CONTROLLER_EMAIL}} rather than testing it against other people's data.

10 Children

TRACES is not directed at children under 16, and the operator does not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child under 16 has created an account on this server, contact {{LEGAL_CONTROLLER_EMAIL}} so it can be reviewed and removed.
